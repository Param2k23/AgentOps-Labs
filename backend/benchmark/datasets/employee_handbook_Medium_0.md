# Aether Corp. Employee Handbook

## About Aether Corp.

Aether Corp. is a leading enterprise solutions provider, committed to **innovation**, **integrity**, and **client success**. Our mission is to empower businesses worldwide through cutting-edge technology and exceptional service. We believe our employees are our greatest asset, and this handbook outlines our policies, expectations, and the benefits we provide to foster a productive, respectful, and secure work environment.

## Employment Policies

*   **Equal Employment Opportunity (EEO):** Aether Corp. is an equal opportunity employer. We are committed to providing a workplace free of discrimination and harassment based on race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity or expression, national origin, age, disability, marital status, veteran status, or any other protected characteristic.
*   **Workplace Conduct:** All employees are expected to conduct themselves professionally, ethically, and respectfully. Harassment, discrimination, bullying, or any form of disruptive behavior will not be tolerated.
*   **Confidentiality & Data Security:** Protecting Aether Corp.'s proprietary information, client data, and intellectual property is paramount. Employees must adhere strictly to confidentiality agreements and data security protocols. Unauthorized disclosure or use of confidential information is prohibited.
*   **Code of Conduct:** Employees must act with honesty, fairness, and transparency in all business dealings, avoiding conflicts of interest and upholding the company's reputation.

## Compensation and Benefits

*   **Pay Periods:** Employees are paid bi-weekly on Friday.
*   **Benefits Package:** Aether Corp. offers a comprehensive benefits package, including:
    *   **Health, Dental, and Vision Insurance**
    *   **401(k) Retirement Plan** with company match
    *   **Life and Disability Insurance**
    *   **Employee Assistance Program (EAP)**
*   **Paid Time Off (PTO):** Aether Corp. provides a combined PTO bank for vacation, sick, and personal leave. Accrual rates increase with tenure.
*   **Holidays:** The company observes 10 paid holidays annually, including New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, and Christmas Day.

## Workplace Environment

*   **Safety & Security:** Aether Corp. is dedicated to maintaining a safe and healthy workplace. Employees must follow all safety procedures and report any hazards, injuries, or security concerns immediately to their manager or HR. Emergency evacuation plans are posted throughout the facilities.
*   **Technology & Communication:** Company-provided equipment and networks are for business use. Incidental personal use is permitted, provided it does not interfere with job duties, violate policies, or compromise security. All electronic communications and data stored on company systems are subject to monitoring.

## Performance & Development

*   **Performance Reviews:** We conduct annual performance reviews to assess accomplishments, provide feedback, and set goals. Continuous feedback and coaching are encouraged throughout the year.
*   **Learning & Development:** Aether Corp. supports continuous learning through internal training programs, external courses, and professional development opportunities to enhance skills and career growth.

## Disciplinary Action & Grievance Procedures

Violations of company policy, poor performance, or misconduct may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination. Aether Corp. utilizes a progressive disciplinary process, tailored to the severity and nature of the infraction. Employees with grievances are encouraged to discuss concerns with their manager or Human Resources.
