## InnovateCorp Information Security Policy

This policy outlines the security requirements and responsibilities for all InnovateCorp employees, contractors, and third parties with access to company information systems and data. Its purpose is to protect InnovateCorp's information assets from unauthorized access, use, disclosure, disruption, modification, or destruction, thereby ensuring business continuity, minimizing risks, and complying with regulatory requirements.

### 1. Information Classification and Handling

InnovateCorp classifies information into distinct categories to ensure appropriate protection:

*   **Public:** Information intended for general public release. No special handling required.
*   **Internal:** Information for internal company use only. Not for unauthorized external distribution.
*   **Confidential:** Sensitive business information (e.g., financial data, client lists, non-public product plans). Access is restricted to authorized personnel. Must be stored in secure systems and encrypted when transmitted externally.
*   **Sensitive/Proprietary:** Highest classification. Includes personally identifiable information (PII), intellectual property, and strategic plans. Access is highly restricted and logged. Requires strong encryption at rest and in transit.

All employees are responsible for correctly classifying and handling information according to these guidelines.

### 2. Access Control

Access to InnovateCorp systems and data is granted based on the principle of least privilege.

*   **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** Access is provisioned based on job function and departmental roles.
*   **Strong Authentication:**
    *   All user accounts must use **strong passwords** (minimum 12 characters, mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols).
    *   **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)** is mandatory for all remote access, privileged accounts, and access to cloud-based services.
*   **Access Reviews:** User access privileges are formally reviewed and validated by data owners/managers **bi-annually**.
*   **Account Locking:** Accounts are locked after a specified number of failed login attempts.

### 3. Endpoint Security

All company-issued devices (laptops, desktops, mobile phones) are subject to these controls.

*   **Anti-Malware Software:** All endpoints must have approved anti-malware software installed and actively running with automatic updates.
*   **Operating System (OS) Updates:** OS and application patches must be applied promptly. Devices must be configured for automatic updates where possible.
*   **Disk Encryption:** Full disk encryption is mandatory for all laptops and mobile devices storing company data.
*   **Mobile Device Management (MDM):** Company-issued mobile devices must be enrolled in InnovateCorp's MDM solution.

### 4. Network Security

InnovateCorp maintains robust network security measures to protect its infrastructure.

*   **Firewalls:** Perimeter and internal firewalls are configured to restrict unauthorized traffic and segment network zones.
*   **Intrusion Detection/Prevention Systems (IDS/IPS):** Deployed to monitor and alert on suspicious network activity.
*   **Secure Wi-Fi:** Corporate Wi-Fi networks use WPA2-Enterprise or WPA3 authentication. Guest networks are isolated from internal resources.
*   **Virtual Private Networks (VPN):** All remote access to InnovateCorp's internal network resources must utilize an approved VPN solution with MFA.

### 5. Incident Response

All security incidents must be reported and handled promptly to minimize impact.

*   **Reporting:** Employees must immediately report any suspected security incidents, breaches, or vulnerabilities to the IT Helpdesk or Security Team.
*   **Response Plan:** InnovateCorp maintains an Incident Response Plan outlining procedures for identification, containment, eradication, recovery, and post-incident analysis.
*   **Communication:** A clear communication protocol is in place for internal and external stakeholders during an incident.

### 6. Employee Responsibilities and Training

Security is a shared responsibility across all InnovateCorp personnel.

*   **Security Awareness Training:** All employees must complete mandatory annual security awareness training.
*   **Phishing Simulations:** Regular phishing simulation exercises will be conducted to enhance employee vigilance.
*   **Clean Desk Policy:** Employees must secure all confidential and sensitive physical documents and remove them from their desks when leaving for an extended period.
*   **Reporting:** Employees are responsible for safeguarding company assets and reporting any observed policy violations or security risks.

### 7. Physical Security

Access to InnovateCorp facilities and critical assets is controlled and monitored.

*   **Facility Access:** All exterior doors and restricted areas require **badge access**.
*   **Visitor Management:** All visitors must sign in, be issued a visitor badge, and be escorted by an InnovateCorp employee at all times.
*   **Server Rooms/Data Centers:** Access is strictly limited to authorized IT and security personnel, requiring specific approvals and access logging. Surveillance cameras are in place.

### 8. Policy Compliance and Review

*   **Enforcement:** Non-compliance with this policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment or contract.
*   **Review:** This policy will be reviewed and updated **annually** by the Security Committee and IT Leadership to ensure its continued effectiveness and relevance.
*   **Audits:** Regular internal and external audits will be conducted to assess compliance with this policy.
