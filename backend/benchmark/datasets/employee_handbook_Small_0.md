### Employee Handbook - Zenith Solutions

#### Welcome & Culture

At Zenith Solutions, we foster an environment of **innovation, integrity, and client success**. We expect all team members to embody our core values, promote a positive workplace, and contribute to our collective growth.

#### Work Hours & Attendance

Our standard operating hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM. Punctuality is essential, and consistent attendance is expected. Employees must notify their direct manager and HR of any planned or unplanned absences as soon as reasonably possible.

#### Code of Conduct

*   **Professionalism**: Treat colleagues, clients, and partners with respect and courtesy.
*   **Anti-Harassment**: Zenith Solutions maintains a **zero-tolerance policy** for harassment or discrimination. Report any concerns immediately to HR.
*   **Ethical Behavior**: Always act with honesty and adhere to company policies and legal requirements.

#### Confidentiality & Data Security

Employees are entrusted with **proprietary company information, client data, and intellectual property**. You are obligated to protect this information from unauthorized disclosure during and after your employment. All company IT resources and networks must be used responsibly and primarily for business purposes.

#### Workplace Safety

Maintain a safe working environment by adhering to all company safety guidelines. Report any workplace hazards or incidents to your manager and HR immediately.

#### Contact & Resources

For all policy questions, concerns, or HR-related matters, please contact Human Resources at hr@zenithsolutions.com. Your direct manager remains your primary resource for day-to-day operational inquiries.
