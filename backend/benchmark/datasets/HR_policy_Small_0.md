# HR Policy Overview

## 1. General Principles
[Fictional Enterprise Name] fosters a **fair, respectful, and productive** work environment, adhering to all **applicable labor laws and regulations**. Our operations are built on integrity and mutual respect.

## 2. Equal Employment Opportunity (EEO)
We provide **equal employment opportunities** to all applicants and employees without regard to race, color, religion, sex (including pregnancy), national origin, age, disability, genetic information, veteran status, or any other legally protected characteristic. Employment decisions are based on **merit, qualifications, and business needs**.

## 3. Workplace Conduct & Professionalism
Employees must conduct themselves **professionally and respectfully**. **Harassment, bullying, discrimination**, or any disrespectful behavior is **strictly prohibited**. All workplace conduct concerns should be reported immediately to management or HR.

## 4. Confidentiality & Data Protection
Employees must **protect company confidential information** (client data, intellectual property, trade secrets). Employee personal data is handled per **applicable data privacy laws**. Unauthorized disclosure or misuse of sensitive information is grounds for disciplinary action.

## 5. Performance & Discipline
Employees are expected to meet **job performance standards** and comply with company policies. Performance issues or policy violations will be addressed through a **fair, consistent disciplinary process**, including warnings, suspension, or termination, depending on severity and frequency.
